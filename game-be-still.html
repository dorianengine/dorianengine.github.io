The audio production of Be still
In this 3 week group project I was the sound designer and music producer/composer.
Ideas and research
During this workshop I followed a series of pillars to stay in the style of the game. I also had multiple games that I analysed and used as references.
Stress
Through the sound design and the music, I dedicated my efforts to making uneasy sound and music to reach that feeling.
The gigantism
Due to the place the game takes place, I used reverb and other effects to emphasize the space and the creepiness around the gigantism of the room.
The childhood
The game is set in a kid’s bedroom so the toys had to be childish… but broken, to be in accord with the vibe of the game.
Sound design
In this video you will be able to hear the sound design that was done.
  • A tragic introduction of the game that helps understand the danger and game mechanics.
  • The end chase cinematic at the end of the game.
The music

Music is always important to me, so trying to make an original horror music was very instructive as it made me do a lot of research that I wouldn’t have done otherwise.

Going from a music that was too cliché to a music that was too memory-like and so out of context for this game, I managed to fit a style of slowness and kept some elements throughout the iterations.
