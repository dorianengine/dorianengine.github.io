The wrong path
This is a fantasy universe I forged over the years and highly evolved thanks to knowledge in narration and worldbuilding.
The universe
The wrong path is a universe inspired by Wakfu, Avatar: the last air bender, Arcane/Runeterra and the guardians of Ga’Hoole by Kathryn Lasky.
The narration

Lots of the narration is inspired by Greek mythology and Christian mythology alongside the references said above.

The power and magic of this world resides in books, created by divinities called “divine writers”. But those divinities and books affected the whole of this planet.

The world building
The map was made using Photoshop and then textured in Substance Painter.
  • Up north, you have a flourishing kingdom named Drehamia with no strict rule, borders, ruler, and a high diversity of land and fauna.
  • Down south is Idraei, a strict empire where factions reside: mines, factories, prison, and the city ruled by the Queen of Goata along with the King of Goata.
  • In the center of the map, the Isil-Ils is where many animals have mutated into humanoid creatures. The most dirigiste race to live there are the “Aelis”.
  • All the way east is Vatiks, a land made from a conflict within the divinities. Only the dead can go to that place and be a part of the black book writers’ dark project.

The creation of the map: the map was first made with a lot of coffee stains put together, then I connected the dots and got the Photoshop version. With time, I was able to expand the map and texture it with Substance Painter.
